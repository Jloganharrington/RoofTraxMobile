Build Spec — Trial Proof Package Page

Feature: Paid trial proof package intake, marketing site Owner: Logan Harrington Version: 1.0 Date: August 11, 2026

0. Scope

Build a public landing page plus authenticated intake flow allowing non-customer contractors to purchase up to three proof packages, submit real claim data, and receive a branded deliverable in 2 business days.

In scope: landing page, account creation, submission wizard, Stripe checkout, file upload, admin review queue, delivery, credit ledger, data purge job.

Out of scope: package generation itself (existing pipeline), AHJ packet build (manual, admin-side), CRM tenant provisioning (existing onboarding flow).

Non-negotiable constraints:

Claim data is purged 30 days after delivery. AHJ jurisdiction work product is retained separately and contains no claim or homeowner data.
Hard cap: 3 packages per company, lifetime.
Submissions gated to jurisdictions with completed AHJ coverage; all others go to waitlist.
No package generates without manual admin approval.

Fields: company name, contact name, business email, phone, license number, license state, company size band, monthly claim band, current CRM (optional).

Validation:

5. Submission wizard — /proof-package/submit

Four steps, progress indicator, autosave draft.

Step 1 — Property & loss: address, city, state, zip, county (autocomplete), date of loss, peril type, roof system, stories.

Step 2 — Claim context: carrier name, internal claim reference, scope notes (free text, 2000 char), uploads.

Uploads: photos (jpg/png/heic, max 40, 15MB each), measurement report (pdf), carrier estimate (pdf/xlsx). Direct-to-object-storage with signed URLs. Show total size.

Step 3 — Branding: logo upload (png/svg, transparent preferred), brand hex color with picker, license number as displayed on documents.

Step 4 — Review & terms: summary of all entries, price shown ($100 if sequence_num = 1, else $65), required checkboxes:

 I am authorized to share this claim and property information.
 I understand this package is prepared for my review and adoption, and that I am responsible for reviewing it before submitting it to any carrier.
 I have read and accept the Trial Package Terms and Privacy Policy.

→ Stripe Checkout. On payment_intent.succeeded: set status paid, increment packages_purchased, credit ledger +amount, set credit_expires_at if first purchase.

6. Admin queue — /admin/trial-queue

Table: company, state/county, sequence #, submitted, status, age. Sort by age. Filter by status.

Detail view shows all intake fields, photo gallery, uploaded documents, account history, AHJ coverage status for the jurisdiction.

Actions: Approve · Reject (with reason + auto-refund) · Mark building · Upload deliverable · Mark delivered · Add notes.

Screening checklist rendered on the detail page (admin confirms before approve):

 Legitimate contractor — license verifiable
 Not a public adjuster or PA firm
 Not in an excluded service area (see §10)
 Real claim data, not fabricated or test content
 AHJ coverage confirmed for jurisdiction
 Photo set sufficient for assessment

Throughput cap: configurable weekly intake limit (default 15). When reached, landing page CTA switches to waitlist mode with: We're at capacity this week. Join the list and we'll open your spot next week.

7. Delivery & notifications
Trigger	Email
Payment succeeded	Receipt + what happens next + expected date (2 business days from approval)
Approved	We're building your package
Rejected	Reason + refund confirmation
Ready	Booking link for a 30-minute walkthrough call — package is NOT attached
Post-call	Deliverable link (signed URL, 30-day expiry)
Day 30 / Day 60	Check-in on the claim + credit balance reminder
Day 83	Your $X credit expires in 7 days

Deliver on the call, not by email. The ready email contains a Calendly-style booking link only. The package link is sent after the walkthrough. This is a deliberate conversion mechanic — do not "simplify" it by attaching the PDF.

Business-day calculation must exclude weekends and US federal holidays.

8. Data retention

Purge job — nightly cron:

For every submission where delivered_at + 30 days <= now() and purged_at is null:

Delete all trial_uploads objects from storage
Null out: property_address, property_city, property_zip, carrier_name, claim_number_ref, scope_notes, date_of_loss
Retain: property_state, county, ahj_jurisdiction, peril_type, roof_system, stories, all timestamps, payment records
Set purged_at = now()
Log to audit table

Retained separately and permanently: the AHJ jurisdiction packet. This is jurisdiction-level work product — code cycle, amendments, permit requirements for a county. It contains no claim data, no homeowner data, no property address. Store it in the AHJ library, keyed to state + county, not to the submission.

State this distinction plainly in the privacy policy. It is the difference between "we keep your claim data" (we don't) and "we keep the code research we did for your county" (we do).

Rejected submissions purge at 7 days.

9. FAQ content

Why does this cost money? Before we build your package we build a complete code packet for your jurisdiction — every applicable code, amendment, and permit requirement for your county. That research is real work, and it's yours.

What happens to my claim data? Photos, addresses, and claim details are deleted 30 days after delivery. We keep the jurisdiction code research, which contains nothing about your claim or your customer.

Can I submit this to the carrier? It's your document, branded to your company, under your license. Review it, adopt it, and submit it exactly as you would anything your own team produced.

How many can I get? Three. After that, the next step is a plan.

What if I subscribe? Everything you spend applies as credit toward an annual plan, Starter or above, for 90 days from your first submission.

Do you work with public adjusters? No. RoofTrax is built for contractors.

Is RoofTrax owned by a roofing company? No. RoofTrax is an independent software company. Its founder has twenty years in insurance restoration, and the platform was built and validated with a restoration contractor as design partner. Every customer is an isolated tenant; no customer can see another customer's data, and we don't use it.

11. Acceptance criteria
 Free-domain email rejected at signup
 Out-of-coverage county routes to waitlist, never to checkout
 4th package attempt blocked with upgrade CTA
 Nothing generates without admin approval
 Rejection triggers automatic Stripe refund
 ready email contains booking link and no package attachment
 Credit ledger reflects every purchase; expiry set from first purchase
 Credit redeemable only against annual plans, Starter and above
 Purge job nulls claim fields and deletes uploads at day 30, retains AHJ record
 Weekly cap flips landing CTA to waitlist mode
 Business-day math excludes weekends and federal holidays
 No prohibited outcome language anywhere in page copy or email templates
 Mobile-responsive; upload flow works from phone (contractors submit from the truck)
