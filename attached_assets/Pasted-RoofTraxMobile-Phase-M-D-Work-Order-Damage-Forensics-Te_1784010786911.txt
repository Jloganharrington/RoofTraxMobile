Standing constraints

App computes nothing derivable — it records raw hit counts and per-hit classifications; it must not compute hail density, per-square severity, or any "significant/approved" judgment. The live hit counter is just a count of records. Significance is the Brain's job.
Evidentiary standards
Full-res original untouched, hashing at capture, triad for damage instances, scale gauge in every per-hit close-up, controlled vocabulary, offline-first. Reuse the shared capture component + C2 recorder — do not fork a second one.
