PROJECT FINANCIALS — CHANGE ORDERS ROW

Three changes to the waterfall in
artifacts/rooftrax-web/src/pages/leads/LeadProfile.tsx.

1 [LOCKED] Remove the "Revised Contract Value" subtotal row from the
   waterfall. It duplicates the Contract Value card at the top of the tab and
   gives the screen two competing "contract value" figures. The waterfall
   reads fine without it:

       Contract Value          $15,000
       Change Orders          + $3,500     ← clickable
       Cost of Goods Sold      ($8,000)
       Job Overhead            ($2,500)
       ──────────────────────────────────
       Net Project Margin        $8,000  (43.2%)

   Keep revised_contract_cents in the API response and the view — it is the
   denominator for net_project_margin_pct and other surfaces will want it.
   This removes a UI row, not a field.

   Instead, surface the revised total on the Contract Value CARD as a
   subline (e.g. "+$3,500 CO · $18,500 revised") so the current contract
   reality is visible in one place rather than two.

2 [LOCKED] The Change Orders row must ALWAYS render, including at $0.
   It is currently gated behind `approvedCoCents !== 0`, so on a job with no
   approved change orders the row vanishes and there is no way to reach the
   list — including to see PENDING change orders awaiting approval, which is
   exactly when someone needs to look. Show "$0" or "—" and keep it clickable.

3 [LOCKED] Clicking the row opens a modal listing that lead's change orders.
   ChangeOrdersPanel already exists in this file (~line 1699) with
   useListPinChangeOrders, approve, and void wired up — reuse it, do not
   write a second list.

   The modal shows ALL change orders with their status, not just approved:
     - approved  → counted in the row total
     - pending   → visible, clearly marked as not yet counted
     - rejected / voided → visible, clearly excluded
   Each entry: description, line items, total, status, signed-document link,
   and the manager-only Approve action already built.

   The ROW total stays approved-only — it must continue to match
   approved_co_cents from the view. The modal is where the fuller picture
   lives. If the row and the modal ever disagree, that is the bug.

4 [LOCKED] Credits must read correctly throughout. Change orders may be
   negative (credits against the job) at both the change-order and
   line-item level. The row already color-codes and signs correctly —
   verify the modal and the totals do too, and that a negative change order
   REDUCES revised contract value rather than being treated as absolute.
