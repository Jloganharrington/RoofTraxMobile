## PART I — CONTEXT

*This section informs tone, copy, and design decisions. It is not site content.*

### 1.1 What RoofTrax is

An independent B2B software company serving storm restoration contractors. The product is a multi-tenant CRM, mobile field inspection app, and canvassing app, built around an AI-assisted **proof package** generation pipeline.

The proof package is not a feature. It is the spine of the system — intake, workflow, documentation, and supplement process all route through it. One claim produces one package.

### 1.2 Entity separation — enforce in all copy

**RoofTrax and NuHome Exteriors, Inc. are separate companies.** NuHome is a design partner and reference customer, not a parent, division, or affiliate.

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| No NuHome branding, letterhead, or license numbers anywhere on the RoofTrax site | |
| Founder credential is framed as **20 years in insurance restoration**, not "a contractor who built software" | |
| NuHome may be named as design partner / first customer, with permission | |
| Data neutrality must be addressed proactively — see FAQ copy in §6.4 | |

**Why this matters:** the ICP is roofing contractors. If prospects perceive RoofTrax as an arm of a competing contractor, they assume their claim data is visible to a rival and refuse to evaluate.

### 1.3 Ideal customer profile

**Primary — the scaling restoration shop.** 5–50 seats, $5M–55M revenue, 15–200+ claims/month, 60%+ insurance restoration. Currently on AccuLynx, JobNimbus, or spreadsheets. Already buying EagleView / Hover / QuickMeasure.

**The pod model** (important for the quote calculator): the operating unit is 1 sales rep + 2 canvassers. A pod produces ~4 claims/week, ~208/year. A rep self-generates ~1/week; each canvasser generates 1–2 signed leads/week.

| Pods | Field seats | Claims/yr | Approx. revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 208 | $4.6M |
| 3 | 9 | 624 | $13.7M |
| 5 | 15 | 1,040 | $22.9M |
| 12 | 36 | 2,496 | $54.9M |

**The defining pain:** the owner or a single supplement manager is the documentation bottleneck. Every package, narrative, and rebuttal routes through one person. Growth is capped by that person's hours, and each new rep makes it worse.

**Anti-ICP — do not sell:** public adjusters and PA firms; pure retail exterior contractors; solo operators with no reps; anyone seeking claim-outcome guarantees; storm chasers with no fixed office.

### 1.4 Positioning

> **RoofTrax turns your newest rep into your best documenter.**
>
> Your 22-year-old field rep produces a forensic proof package indistinguishable from one written by a 20-year supplement manager — from their phone, at the property, before they leave the driveway.

**Category argument:** AccuLynx and JobNimbus are job-management systems. They track where a job is; they don't make it win. RoofTrax is a documentation and evidence system with job management attached.

**Competitive framing:**

| Against | Line |
|---|---|
| AccuLynx / JobNimbus | "They track the job. We build the file. And we don't charge you for hiring." |
| Spreadsheets + Dropbox | "Your process lives in one person's head. What happens when they leave?" |
| EagleView / Hover | Not a competitor — an input. Same price per claim, vastly more output. |
| Hiring a supplement manager | "$85–120K salary, or $50 a claim and every rep has the capability." |

## PART II — PRICING MODEL (AUTHORITATIVE)

### 2.1 The model

**Seats are unlimited and free. Customers pay per claim.**

Annual commitment on claim volume, banked across the term, graduated band pricing.

| Band (claims/year) | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1 – 500 | $50 |
| 501 – 1,500 | $45 |
| 1,501 – 3,000 | $40 |
| 3,001+ | $35 |

**Graduated, not cliff.** A customer at 600 claims pays $50 on the first 500 and $45 on the next 100. There is no volume at which buying more increases total price.

### 2.2 Why $50 per claim

Contractors already pay **$30–70 per claim for a roof measurement report** — geometry alone. This is the anchor and it belongs on the pricing page.

> *One gets you geometry. The other gets you the file.*

At $50/claim a customer pays ~0.23% of their revenue — below the 0.5–2% vertical-SaaS norm. Deliberate land-grab pricing, not a ceiling.

### 2.3 Plan ladder

| Plan | Claims/yr | Annual | Monthly equiv. | Eff. $/claim | Typical firm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | 150 | $7,500 | $625 | $50.00 | 1 pod, ~$3.3M |
| **Crew** | 400 | $20,000 | $1,667 | $50.00 | 2 pods, ~$8.8M |
| **Team** | 900 | $43,000 | $3,583 | $47.78 | 4–5 pods, ~$20M |
| **Fleet** | 2,000 | $90,000 | $7,500 | $45.00 | 9–10 pods, ~$44M |
| **Regional** | 4,000 | $165,000 | $13,750 | $41.25 | 19+ pods, ~$88M |

Custom commitments permitted at any volume; the band function governs.

### 2.4 Billing terms

| Term | Multiplier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual prepaid** | 1.00 | Paid upfront, full allotment immediately available |
| **Quarterly** | 1.10 | Annual commitment, four installments |
| **Monthly** | 1.25 | Annual commitment, twelve installments |

**Banking:** committed claims bank across the full annual term — not monthly buckets. Storm restoration is seasonal; every incumbent uses monthly expiry. This is a marketed differentiator.

**Expiry:** banked claims expire 24 months from purchase, and require an active subscription to redeem.

**Overage:** current band rate + $10. Notifications at 80% and 100%. Priced to trigger an upgrade conversation, not to punish.

**True-up:** annual, upward only. No mid-term reduction — banking absorbs seasonal downturns.

### 2.5 Setup fees

**Never waived.** Annual billing earns a reduced rate.

| Plan | Annual | Quarterly / Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | $495 | $795 |
| Crew | $1,495 | $2,295 |
| Team | $2,995 | $4,495 |
| Fleet | $5,995 | $8,995 |
| Regional | $9,995 | $14,995 |

Covers: CRM data migration, price book load, AHJ library build for every jurisdiction worked, role and template configuration, team training. 6–7% of first-year contract value.

**Objection-handler copy:**
> Setup isn't a fee we invented. We migrate your existing CRM, load your price book, build the code library for every jurisdiction you work in, and configure your roles and templates. Annual customers pay less because we can schedule that work more efficiently.

### 2.6 Feature tiers

Independent of volume — a small firm may need enterprise features; a large one may not.

| | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | CRM, mobile inspection, canvassing app, proof packages, AHJ library, supplement documents | Included |
| **Professional** | + API access, custom roles, priority AHJ requests, advanced reporting | +$249/mo |
| **Enterprise** | + SSO/SAML, multi-office hierarchy, dedicated CSM, uptime SLA, quarterly business review | +$999/mo |

### 2.7 Metering rules — critical

**A claim is consumed when a proof package reaches `delivered` status.** Not at claim creation, not at draft.

- **Regeneration, revisions, and corrections on an existing package: FREE.** Never re-consume.
- **Supplement documents on an existing claim: FREE.**
- Voided or duplicate claims: admin-reversible with audit log.
- Consumption order: commitment → bank → overage.

Charging for revisions would recreate the rationing behavior this entire model exists to prevent. This is the single most important implementation rule in the document.

**Band function (authoritative):**

```
def annual_price(claims):
    bands = [(500, 5000), (1000, 4500), (1500, 4000), (INF, 3500)]  # cents
    remaining, total = claims, 0
    for width, rate in bands:
        take = min(remaining, width)
        total += take * rate
        remaining -= take
        if remaining <= 0: break
    return total
```

## PART IV — TRIAL PROGRAM

### 4.1 Model

Paid trial. Contractor submits a real claim; RoofTrax builds the AHJ packet for their jurisdiction and produces a proof package branded to their company, delivered in 2 business days on a live walkthrough call.

| | |
|---|---|
| First package | $100 |
| Second and third | $65 each |
| Maximum | 3 per company, lifetime |
| Credit | 100% toward annual prepaid plans, **Crew and above**, within 90 days of first submission |
| Turnaround | 2 business days from approval |
| Delivery | 30-minute walkthrough call — **package is not emailed in advance** |

**Why paid:** free trials produce polite feedback and near-zero usage. Paid submissions come from contractors with a real claim and real intent.

**Why the AHJ packet matters:** it is retained as jurisdiction-level work product, keyed to state + county. It contains no claim or homeowner data. This turns the trial pipeline into a demand-ranked map for AHJ library expansion — you build coverage where contractors are actively raising their hands, and onboarding for that county becomes instant afterward.

### 4.2 Routes and flow

```
/proof-package          landing
  → /proof-package/start        account creation + coverage gate
    → /proof-package/submit     4-step wizard
      → Stripe checkout
        → admin review queue (manual approval required)
          → build → ready → booking link → call → delivery
```

### 4.3 Account creation

Fields: company name, contact name, business email, phone, license number, license state, company size band, monthly claim band, current CRM (optional).

**Validation:**
- Reject free email domains (gmail, yahoo, outlook, hotmail, aol, icloud, proton): `Please use your company email address.`
- License number required.
- Email verification before submission is permitted.

**Coverage gate:** check state + county against `ahj_coverage`.
- `covered` → proceed
- otherwise → `/proof-package/waitlist`: `We haven't finished the code library for [County], [ST] yet. Add your name and we'll reach out as soon as it's ready.`

**Cap:** if `packages_purchased >= 3`, block: `You've used all three trial packages. The next step is a plan.` → `/pricing`

### 4.4 Submission wizard

**Step 1 — Property & loss:** address, city, state, zip, county (autocomplete), date of loss, peril type, roof system, stories.

**Step 2 — Claim context:** carrier name, internal claim reference, scope notes (2000 char), uploads — photos (jpg/png/heic, max 40, 15MB each), measurement report (pdf), carrier estimate (pdf/xlsx). Direct-to-storage via signed URLs.

**Step 3 — Branding:** logo (png/svg), brand hex color, license number as displayed.

**Step 4 — Review & terms.** Price shown ($100 if first, else $65). Required checkboxes:

- [ ] I am authorized to share this claim and property information.
- [ ] I understand this package is prepared for my review and adoption, and I am responsible for reviewing it before submitting it to any carrier.
- [ ] I have read and accept the Trial Package Terms and Privacy Policy.

**Status flow:**
```
draft → paid → in_review → approved → building → ready → delivered
                    ↓
                 rejected (auto-refund)
```

### 4.5 Admin queue

`/admin/trial-queue` — table sorted by age: company, state/county, sequence #, submitted, status.

Detail view: all intake fields, photo gallery, documents, account history, AHJ coverage status.

**Screening checklist, rendered on the detail page, confirmed before approve:**
- [ ] Legitimate contractor, license verifiable
- [ ] Not a public adjuster or PA firm
- [ ] Not in an excluded service area
- [ ] Real claim data, not fabricated
- [ ] AHJ coverage confirmed
- [ ] Photo set sufficient for assessment

**Nothing generates without manual approval.**

**Throughput cap:** configurable weekly intake limit (default 15). At cap, landing CTA switches to waitlist: `We're at capacity this week. Join the list and we'll open your spot next week.`

### 4.6 Email sequence

| Trigger | Content |
|---|---|
| Payment succeeded | Receipt, what happens next, expected date |
| Approved | "We're building your package" |
| Rejected | Reason + refund confirmation |
| **Ready** | **Booking link only — no package attached** |
| Post-call | Deliverable link, signed URL, 30-day expiry |
| Day 30 / 60 | Check-in + credit balance reminder |
| Day 83 | "Your $X credit expires in 7 days" |

**The `ready` email must not attach the package.** Delivery happens on the call. This is a deliberate conversion mechanic — do not "simplify" it.

Business-day math excludes weekends and US federal holidays.

### 4.7 Data retention

**Nightly purge job.** For submissions where `delivered_at + 30 days <= now()` and `purged_at is null`:

1. Delete all upload objects from storage
2. Null: `property_address`, `property_city`, `property_zip`, `carrier_name`, `claim_number_ref`, `scope_notes`, `date_of_loss`
3. **Retain:** `property_state`, `county`, `ahj_jurisdiction`, `peril_type`, `roof_system`, `stories`, timestamps, payment records
4. Set `purged_at`, log to audit table

Rejected submissions purge at 7 days.

**Retained separately and permanently:** the AHJ jurisdiction packet, in the AHJ library keyed to state + county. Contains no claim data, no homeowner data, no property address.

State this distinction plainly in the privacy policy. It is the difference between "we keep your claim data" (we don't) and "we keep the code research we did for your county" (we do).

## PART VI — COPY RULES & COMPLIANCE

### 6.1 Prohibited claims — hard constraint

**Never claim, guarantee, or imply influence over coverage determinations, carrier payment amounts, or claim approval.**

| Prohibited | Permitted |
|---|---|
| Approval rates | Documentation quality, completeness, consistency |
| Recovery or settlement amounts | Code citation accuracy |
| "We get claims paid" | Speed and turnaround |
| Testimonials phrased as payment outcomes | Rep-level capability |
| Any implied carrier influence | Close rate on inspected leads *(their sales metric — safe)* |
| Specific throughput % before beta data | "Built to increase file throughput" |

Every deliverable is produced for the contractor's review and adoption, submitted under their own license. State this in the FAQ and terms.

This is not conservatism — the entire product architecture depends on contractor-lane discipline, and marketing that implies claim influence creates regulatory exposure in states with stricter public-adjusting statutes than Virginia's.

### 6.2 Required disclosures

- Trial credit: **both conditions always shown together** — "annual plans, Crew and above, within 90 days of your first trial submission." A contractor who buys three packages assuming credit applies to Solo and learns otherwise at checkout has a legitimate grievance.
- Setup fee: shown on every quote, never presented as waivable.
- Data retention: purge policy stated plainly in privacy policy and trial terms.
- Three-package lifetime cap: stated at first purchase, not discovered at the fourth attempt.

### 6.3 Tone

Direct, operational, unembellished. The audience runs crews and argues with adjusters; they detect marketing language instantly and discount it. Short sentences. Concrete numbers. No superlatives.

Write like an operator explaining a system to another operator.

### 6.4 FAQ copy

**Why per claim instead of per seat?**
Because seats aren't the work — claims are. Hire ten canvassers or a hundred; we don't charge you for growing. You pay for the files you build.

**What does a claim cost?**
$50, coming down with volume. About what you already pay for a measurement report.

**What if I don't use my claims?**
They bank across your whole term. Storm season isn't twelve months long and we don't pretend it is.

**Do revisions cost extra?**
No. Regenerate, revise, and add supplement documents on a claim as many times as you need. You're charged once, when the package is delivered.

**Why is there a setup fee?**
We migrate your CRM, load your price book, build the code library for every jurisdiction you work in, and train your team. That's real work, and it's why you're productive in two weeks instead of six months.

**Is RoofTrax owned by a roofing company?**
No. RoofTrax is an independent software company. Its founder has twenty years in insurance restoration, and the platform was built and validated with a restoration contractor as design partner. Every customer is an isolated tenant — no customer can see another's data, and we don't use it.

**What happens to my claim data on a trial?**
Photos, addresses, and claim details are deleted 30 days after delivery. We keep the jurisdiction code research, which contains nothing about your claim or your customer.

**Can I submit the package to the carrier?**
It's your document, branded to your company, under your license. Review it, adopt it, and submit it exactly as you would anything your own team produced.

**Do you work with public adjusters?**
No. RoofTrax is built for contractors.

## APPENDIX A — INTERNAL PROJECTIONS

*Not site content. Context for sizing decisions.*

**ARR at 350 customers:**

| Scenario | Mix (Solo/Crew/Team/Fleet/Regional) | ARR | Avg ACV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 40/35/18/6/1 % | $8.75M | $24,997 |
| Base | 30/35/23/10/2 % | $10.97M | $31,350 |
| Optimistic | 20/34/28/14/4 % | $13.84M | $39,540 |

Gross margin 98.4% across all scenarios. Package COGS ($0.75) is immaterial; storage and support are the real cost drivers.

**Versus prior models:** original per-package $2.36M; seat-based $2.52M. The increase is not a price rise in the customer's eyes — it corrects a meter that was measuring the wrong thing.

**Implication:** conservative mix reaches $3M ARR at roughly **120 customers**, not 350. The acquisition target drops sharply, CAC ceiling rises with ACV, and onboarding capacity becomes the binding constraint — each lost customer now costs $25K in ACV rather than $7K.
