What this is. Aluminum siding currently dead-ends at a static "no simulated repair" card — no capture, no record, no report output. This adds the record end to end.

The idea the whole thing rests on: the ASP is non-destructive. No panel removed, no fastener drawn, no coating cut, no simulated repair. Aluminum deforms plastically, so manipulation answers nothing a product analysis doesn't, and it would produce damage indistinguishable from what the assessment measures. The record therefore carries no manipulation outcomes and supports no finding about how a panel, nailing hem, or interlock would respond to manipulation. RAP (asphalt) and VAP (vinyl) are built entirely around that question; ASP is not. Any field, label, comment, or report sentence implying a manipulation outcome is a defect — flag it, don't write it.

Docs are NOT in scope. Do not create anything under boiler-plate-docs/. Step 6 only adds the section key so the Library slot exists.
