Build Spec — Pricing, Setup Fees & Enterprise Calculator

Feature: Pricing page, quote engine, and checkout pricing logic Owner: Logan Harrington Version: 1.0 Date: August 11, 2026 Related: TRIAL-PACKAGE-PAGE-SPEC.md, RoofTrax GTM Plan v1.1

0. Scope

Implement the full subscription pricing model: five tiers, tiered setup fees, an Enterprise seat-band calculator, and the credit redemption rule from the trial program.

Non-negotiable rules:

Setup fees are never waived. Annual billing earns a discounted setup fee, not a free one.
Field tier is annual only. No monthly option.
Trial credit applies only to annual plans, Starter and above.
Enterprise bills on seats filled (activated), not seats licensed, subject to an annual minimum commit.

2. Setup fees

Charged at signup, in addition to the first subscription invoice. Never waived.

Tier	Monthly billing	Annual billing
Field	—	$295
Starter	$1,295	$795
Pro	$2,995	$1,995
Scale	$5,995	$4,995
Enterprise	$120 / seat filled	$75 / seat filled

Enterprise setup fee cap: $30,000 monthly / $20,000 annual. Past roughly 270 seats the fee stops tracking real implementation work — provisioning becomes bulk, not per-seat.

Page copy for the setup fee (use close to verbatim — this is the objection handler):

Setup isn't a fee we invented. We migrate your existing CRM data, load your price book, build the code library for every jurisdiction you work in, and configure your roles and templates. Annual customers pay a reduced rate because we're able to schedule that work more efficiently.

3. Enterprise calculator
Formula
enterprise_mrr_annual(seats) =
    1700
  + min(max(seats - 50, 0), 100) * 20      // seats  51–150
  + min(max(seats - 150, 0), 150) * 15     // seats 151–300
  + max(seats - 300, 0) * 11               // seats  301+

enterprise_mrr_monthly(seats) = ceil(enterprise_mrr_annual(seats) * 1.20)

enterprise_acv(seats) = enterprise_mrr_annual(seats) * 12

The $1,700 base covers the Enterprise feature set (§3.3) and the first 50 seats. Enterprise begins at 51 seats; below that, quote Scale.

3.3 Enterprise feature set

The base fee must map to deliverables. Enterprise includes everything in Scale, plus: SSO/SAML, multi-office hierarchy and reporting, custom role definitions, API access, dedicated Customer Success Manager, uptime SLA, quarterly business review, and priority AHJ jurisdiction requests.

3.4 Seats filled vs. licensed

Enterprise bills on activated seats — a seat with a logged-in user in the billing period.

Annual minimum commit is set at signup based on initial seat count. Billing never falls below it during the term.
Quarterly true-up, upward only. Seats added above commit are billed at the applicable band rate, prorated to term end.
No downward adjustment mid-term. Seasonal reductions do not reduce the commit; the commit resets at renewal.

Setup fee at signup is calculated on the initial committed seat count. Seats added later incur no additional setup fee.

4. Trial credit redemption

From TRIAL-PACKAGE-PAGE-SPEC.md. Credit is earned on trial package purchases ($100 first, $65 second and third — max $230).

Eligibility rules — enforce in checkout:

Condition	Result
Annual billing + Starter or above	Credit applies in full
Annual billing + Field	Not eligible
Monthly billing, any tier	Not eligible
More than 90 days from first trial purchase	Expired

Credit applies to the subscription line only, never to the setup fee.

Required display copy at checkout and on the pricing page:

Trial package credit applies to annual plans, Starter and above, within 90 days of your first trial submission.

Both conditions must appear together wherever credit is mentioned. A prospect who buys three packages assuming credit applies to Field and discovers otherwise at checkout is a legitimate grievance.

5. Quote calculator UI

Public component on the pricing page.

Inputs: number of seats (slider or numeric, 1–600); billing preference (Annual / Monthly toggle); estimated packages per month (optional, drives tier recommendation).

Behavior:

Auto-recommend a tier from seat count: 1–3 Field, 4–8 Starter, 9–20 Pro, 21–50 Scale, 51+ Enterprise
If Monthly is selected and recommended tier is Field, show: Field is available on annual billing only. Here's Starter monthly. and shift the recommendation.
Enterprise (51+ seats) shows a calculated estimate plus a Talk to us CTA — do not allow Enterprise self-checkout.

Output block must show, as separate line items:

Subscription        $X,XXX / mo   ($XX,XXX billed annually)
Setup (one-time)    $X,XXX
─────────────────────────────────
First-year total    $XX,XXX

Never display a blended or bundled figure. Setup and subscription stay visually distinct.

Annual/Monthly toggle behavior: when the user switches to Monthly, show the delta explicitly — both the higher subscription rate and the higher setup fee. The comparison is the sales argument; do not hide it.

7. Acceptance criteria
 Field tier offers no monthly option anywhere in UI or API
 Setup fee appears on every quote and checkout; no code path waives it
 Enterprise formula matches all §3.2 reference values exactly
 Enterprise setup fee caps at $20,000 annual / $30,000 monthly
 Enterprise self-checkout blocked; routes to contact form
 Trial credit rejected on Field, rejected on monthly, rejected past 90 days
 Trial credit never reduces the setup fee line
 Credit eligibility copy displays both conditions together, everywhere credit is mentioned
 Quote output shows subscription and setup as separate line items
 Annual/Monthly toggle shows the delta on both subscription and setup
 Enterprise true-up runs quarterly, upward only, never reduces below commit
 All pricing values read from tables, not constants
8. Notes for future revision

Do not build these as assumptions — build the mechanism to change them.

Setup fees run 29–32% of first-year ACV at every tier. That is internally consistent but above the typical vertical-SaaS range of 10–25%. Watch Field specifically: $295 on a $996 subscription is a 30% add at the most price-sensitive point in the book. If beta conversion suppresses at Field, soften Field before touching the others.

Enterprise floor. The GTM plan states a $2,900/mo Enterprise floor. This formula reaches that around 110 seats. The formula governs; treat 51 seats as the Enterprise boundary and revise the GTM figure at the next version.

Per-seat consistency. Existing tiers land at $26–29/seat/mo on annual. The Enterprise bands continue that curve downward. Preserve this relationship if tiers are repriced — it is what makes the ladder feel coherent to a buyer comparing options.
