═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THIS WIDGET IS FOR [read before designing]
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Every other surface in this app shows CURRENT STATE. The pipelines show where
each lead sits. This widget answers a different question that nothing else
answers: WHAT IS STUCK, WHO OWNS IT, AND HOW LONG HAS IT BEEN THAT WAY.

A lead can sit in follow_up indefinitely and nothing tells anyone. That is the
gap. Rank by cost of delay, not alphabetically and not by recency.

Manager+ only (already gated — requireWidgetCapability('action_required')).
Company-scoped: a manager sees the whole company (role rank, no reporting
tree — there is no "their team" in this schema).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1 [LOCKED] Data sources — use ONLY what exists
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Do NOT create new tables or columns for this widget. Derive from:

  a) OVERDUE LOOP STAGES — the primary source.
     pins.pipelineStage is a loop stage AND pins.loopNextActionAt < now().
     There are 12 loop stages across the three pipelines; get the authoritative
     list from artifacts/api-server/src/lib/pipelineStages.ts by filtering
     isLoopStage === true. Do NOT hardcode a stage list — read it from the
     stage vocabulary so it stays correct when stages change.
     Overdue-ness = now() - loopNextActionAt.

  b) STALLED IN STAGE — pins where stageEnteredAt is old and the stage is
     NOT terminal. Use this to catch leads rotting in a non-loop stage that
     has no loopNextActionAt set at all. Pick a sane threshold and state what
     you chose and why in your report.

  c) BLOCKED CLAIMS — inspections that started but cannot progress:
     - status 'capturing' with no recent activity (field work started, stalled)
     - status 'validating' sitting unresolved
     - phase 'preliminary' complete but no signed_agreements row (FIPSA
       unsigned — the claim cannot proceed to forensic without it)
     Use the INSPECTION_STATUSES vocabulary ('scheduled','capturing',
     'validating','submitted','package_ready') and INSPECTION_PHASES
     ('preliminary','forensic'). Do not invent status values.

  d) NEEDS STAGE REVIEW — pins.needsStageReview === true. These were
     auto-mapped during a migration and a human was supposed to confirm them.

  There is NO CFR / 21-day-clock field in this schema. Do not build one and
  do not fake it. If you believe such a deadline should be tracked, say so in
  your report as a recommendation — do not add schema for it in this task.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3 [REASONING OK] Ranking — this is the judgment call
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Cost of delay" is yours to define. Considerations, not rules:
  - Days overdue matters, but not linearly — something 30 days overdue is not
    30x worse than 1 day.
  - Stage position matters. A lead stuck at contract_pending or
    supplement_dispute is closer to money than one stuck at appt_scheduled.
  - Blocked claims where field work is already DONE (validating, or captured-
    but-unsigned FIPSA) represent sunk effort about to be wasted — weight
    accordingly.
  - needsStageReview is data hygiene, not urgency — it should rank below real
    operational blockage.

Explain your ranking formula in your report. If it needs tuning later, that is
fine — state the reasoning so it can be argued with.
