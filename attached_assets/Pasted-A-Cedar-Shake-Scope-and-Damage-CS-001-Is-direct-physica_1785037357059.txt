## Shared Validation Rules for Cedar and Standing Seam ```text 1. Do not allow “documented evidence does not support a reliable spot repair” unless: - At least two basis factors are selected; and - At least one is direct-test evidence or product/material evidence. 2. Do not allow: - “Discontinued confirmed” without linked manufacturer/distributor evidence. - “No sufficient quantity located” without a documented availability search. - “Adjacent material damaged during test” without linked test media. - “Could not reset/reseam securely” without a test record or manufacturer instruction. - “Substitute incompatible” without a documented comparison. 3. Do not allow any output phrase: - Full replacement required - Carrier must replace - Insurance owes - Code requires replacement - Material cannot be repaired 4. Temporary weather protection must only be available when: - Emergency mitigation is selected; or - A controlled test created an exposed or non-weather-resistant condition. 5. Keep Cedar Shake and Standing Seam determinations separate from Asphalt Shingle determinations. Do not reuse shingle-specific questions, terminology, or repair logic for cedar or standing seam systems.
