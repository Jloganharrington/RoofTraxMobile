Vinyl Siding Repairability Assessment
Siding Type
Vinyl Siding → Open Vinyl Repairability Assessment
Aluminum Siding → Route to the Product ID–supported non-repairability document; no simulated repair flow unless the Product ID determination specifically supports one.
Instructions
Identify an already damaged vinyl siding panel to be removed.
Mark the damaged panel with an “X.”
Mark the panel directly above X as 1.
Mark the panel directly below X as 2.
Mark any adjacent panel, lap, J-channel, corner post, or trim component that must be manipulated as 3, 4, T1, T2, as applicable.
Take a clear photograph of the complete marked repair zone.
Save as: VAP1 — Marked Vinyl Repair Zone / Pre-Manipulation Baseline.

Simulated Repair Procedure
Use a siding unlock/zip tool to release Panel 1 from Panel X.
Locate and gently remove all fasteners securing Panel X.
Release Panel X from the lower locking edge and applicable lap or trim interfaces.
Remove Panel X.
Install a compatible replacement panel in X’s original location.
Re-engage the lower locking edge and Panel 1.
Refasten through the proper nailing hem only. Do not face-nail the replacement panel.
Confirm all manipulated panels and trim components are properly seated, aligned, locked, and able to move normally.

Report Output
Include:

VAP1 — marked pre-manipulation repair-zone photo.
Up to two newly damaged-panel or component photos.
Photo priority:

A broken, stretched, or non-reengageable locking edge/lap joint.
A cracked, split, torn, or broken surrounding vinyl panel.
A trim-interface or nail-hem failure if it is the strongest documented result.
This keeps the siding protocol as simple as the asphalt flow: mark → baseline photo → normal removal/replacement → annotate collateral effects → unique-component scorecard.
